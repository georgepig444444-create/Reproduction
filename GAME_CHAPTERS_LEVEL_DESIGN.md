# Human Life Development Game – Chapter/Level Design

## Overview
This document defines the educational flow for an interactive, science-documentary-style learning experience that follows human life development from sperm production to birth.

---

## Level 1: Sperm Production
**Location:** Testes

### Player Experience
- Start in a **microscopic view** inside the testes.
- Show simplified animation of sperm formation.
- Highlight **seminiferous tubules** and basic maturation flow.
- Transition with a smooth zoom-out to indicate anatomical position in the male body.

### Learning Goals
- Understand where sperm are produced.
- Recognize seminiferous tubules as the production site.

---

## Level 2: Transport Pathway

### Route
- Epididymis
- Vas deferens

### Player Experience
- Follow sperm movement through each structure.
- Visualize maturation changes (motility/tail movement readiness).
- Trigger educational popups explaining each organ’s function.

### Learning Goals
- Explain sperm transport sequence.
- Identify sperm maturation during transit.

---

## Level 3: Entering the Female Reproductive System

### Structures Shown
- Uterus
- Fallopian tube

### Player Experience
- Use a documentary-style zoom navigation from body-level to cellular-level.
- Display many sperm, while tracking one “hero” sperm as the narrative anchor.

### Learning Goals
- Locate key female reproductive structures.
- Understand sperm migration path toward the ovum.

---

## Level 4: Fertilization

### Player Experience
- Show sperm-egg encounter via **cell-level medical diagram animation**.
- Include scientific explainers for:
  - fusion of genetic material
  - zygote formation
- Keep visuals strictly non-graphic and educational.

### Learning Goals
- Describe the biological meaning of fertilization.
- Define the zygote as the first post-fertilization stage.

---

## Level 5: Cell Division & Early Development

### Development Sequence
- Zygote → Morula → Blastocyst

### Player Experience
- Animate early cleavage stages in simplified diagram form.
- Zoom out to show blastocyst implantation into the uterine wall.

### Learning Goals
- Recognize early embryonic developmental milestones.
- Understand implantation in context.

---

## Level 6: Fetal Development

### Time Progression
- 1st trimester
- 2nd trimester
- 3rd trimester

### Player Experience
- Time-lapse progression across trimesters.
- Simplified organ development callouts for each stage.

### Learning Goals
- Compare trimester-specific developmental changes.
- Connect organ development to timeline.

---

## Final Level: Birth

### Player Experience
- Transition to childbirth outcome in a **medical illustration style**.
- Avoid graphic imagery; focus on educational schematic representation.
- End sequence text: **“Human Life Development Complete”**.

### Learning Goals
- Understand birth as the culmination of fetal development.

---

## Cross-Level Features

### 1) View Modes
- Toggle between:
  - Microscopic view
  - Full body anatomical view

### 2) Quiz Mode
- Short formative quiz after each level.
- Suggested format:
  - 3–5 questions
  - Instant feedback
  - Retry option for reinforcement

### 3) Interactive Labels
- Tap/click labels for definitions and function summaries.
- Optional pronunciation support for anatomical terms.

### 4) Pause + Explore Mode
- Pause main sequence at any time.
- Freely inspect structures, read labels, and replay short animations.

---

## Content Safety / Presentation Rules
- Educational and clinical tone only.
- No explicit or graphic depiction.
- Use diagrammatic, age-appropriate medical visuals.
- Prefer neutral color palettes and clear annotation.
